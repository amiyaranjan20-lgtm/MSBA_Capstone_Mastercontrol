# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) – Image Collection

This folder contains the visualizations generated during the exploratory data analysis (EDA) phase of the **MasterControl MSBA Capstone Project**.

These images were created to support the initial investigation of the lead dataset and to better understand patterns that influence **lead progression for the Mx product line**.

The visualizations helped explore:

- The overall **structure and distribution of the lead dataset**
- Differences in **progression rates between Qx and Mx products**
- Distribution of leads across **industries, company sizes, and manufacturing models**
- Patterns related to **job titles, seniority levels, and buyer roles**
- Potential relationships between **account characteristics and lead progression**
- Correlations between key variables in the dataset

These EDA visuals were important for identifying trends such as **imbalances in progression outcomes, influential industry segments, and potential predictors of lead success**.

Insights derived from these visualizations guided later stages of the project, including:

- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Feature selection and feature engineering
- Development of predictive models for lead progression

Overall, this image collection documents the exploratory analysis that helped shape the analytical approach used in the MasterControl Capstone project.
